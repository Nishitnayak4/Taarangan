About Taarangan 2k25

Taarangan is the eagerly awaited Annual Inter-Collegiate Cultural Festival of Thakur College of Science and Commerce(Autonomous). Now in its 18th year, Taarangan 2k25 embraces the Marvel Multiverse theme, bringing together students from across the country to celebrate creativity, talent, and culture. The fest features electrifying performances, thrilling competitions, workshops, and celebrity appearances, making it a spectacular event for all participants and attendees.





Theme 2025: Marvel Multiverse Madness

Step into the Marvel Multiverse — where every world collides in creativity, energy, and innovation! Taarangan 2k25 celebrates the limitless power of imagination through cosmic performances, heroic talents, and multiversal vibes. Be ready to experience chaos, power, and unity like never before!

About the
College

Founded in 1992, Thakur College of Science and Commerce [TCSC] has grown from a small cohort of 57 students to a reputed institution with over 15,000 learners. With more than 25 years of excellence, it is recognized for quality education and holistic development through academics and extracurricular activities. The institution thrives with one key approach – a student friendly environment and prioritizing overall development. It stands as a leading centre of education, dedicated to shaping future–ready individuals.
