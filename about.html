About Taarangan 2k25

Thakur College of Science and Commerce, renowned for its illustrious inter-collegiate fest – Taarangan has hosted numerous luminant personalities each year with its exciting endeavours. Taarangan 2025–2026 acknowledges the aim to reach even greater heights and achieve more excellent feats with our most prized possession – our teams and allies. Tasarangan’s 18 years of cultural excellence spell a tale of evergreen excitement experienced by pupils beaming with talent in a night graced by the youth zeal and culture, serving as an elevation to the ever rising spirit of the extra-ordinary fest.





Theme 2025: Marvel Multiverse Madness

The theme for Taarangan 2025–26 surrounds around the magic of the multiverse — Bringing every superhero to the Taarangan Realm, Multiverse—K—Mahanayaks celebrates the spirit of the superheroes. Our theme this year is a celebration of the universe’s extraordinary saviours — the 18th year of Taarangan strives to reach infinity and beyond!

About the
College

Founded in 1992, Thakur College of Science and Commerce [TCSC] has grown from a small cohort of 57 students to a reputed institution with over 15,000 learners. With more than 25 years of excellence, it is recognized for quality education and holistic development through academics and extracurricular activities. The institution thrives with one key approach – a student friendly environment and prioritizing overall development. It stands as a leading centre of education, dedicated to shaping future–ready individuals.
